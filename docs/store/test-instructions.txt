No account needed. After installing, open this video in a new tab (tabs opened before the install need a reload): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jNQXAC9IVRw. Dismiss a cookie notice if shown. After a few seconds a collapsed "Transcript" card appears above the recommendations (below the video in narrow windows) and its Copy button becomes active. Click Copy, or expand the card and click a line to seek. The toolbar popup sets the view and two options.
