### Body

```text
I use YouTube transcripts quite a lot. Sometimes the topic interests me, but I'd rather read the text than watch the whole video.

I tried other tools and kept running into the same frustration: I wanted a simple transcript card that looked like part of YouTube. So I made YTTS for myself and then released it as a free, open-source Chrome extension.

The two priorities are straightforward: keep the design consistent with YouTube, in light and dark mode, and keep it completely free, with no ads or account.

It reads YouTube's existing transcript, gives you lines, sentences or text views, and copies the whole thing with optional timestamps. It doesn't generate transcripts or AI summaries.

I also wanted to have a public project that people can actually use. Most of my other work is private, and as a freelancer I'd like my public work to show more than a commit graph.

Demo: https://ytts.christophel.io/
Source: https://github.com/maphel/YTTS

If you regularly use transcripts, what feels awkward about your current workflow?
```
