## Short description

```text
Read and copy YouTube transcripts in a card that matches YouTube's light and dark themes.
```

## Description

Keine URLs in dieses Feld kopieren; die Linkfelder unten verwenden.

```text
YTTS is a free, open-source Chromium extension that places a transcript card directly on the YouTube watch page. The card follows YouTube's light and dark themes and interface language, keeping transcripts close to the video.

Read the transcript as caption lines, grouped sentences, or continuous text. Copy the full transcript with one click, with optional timestamps, or click a timestamp to jump to that point in the video. You can also choose whether the card opens expanded by default.

YTTS has no advertising, analytics, subscription, or required account. Transcript processing happens locally in your browser. The extension does not send transcript or viewing data to its developer. It is open source under the MIT license.

The interface supports 21 locales, including right-to-left Arabic. Videos must already have a transcript available through YouTube. YTTS displays the transcript provided by YouTube; it does not generate captions, translate transcripts, or create AI summaries.

YTTS is an independent project and is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.
```

## Als Alternative vorschlagen

Nach Aufnahme auf der jeweiligen Produktseite **Contribute to this page → Suggest Alternatives**
verwenden. Sachliche Überschneidungen nennen; keine Konkurrenz bewerten, die du nicht selbst
getestet hast.

1. [Ditto Transcript Generator](https://alternativeto.net/software/ditto-transcript-generator/about/):
   lokale Open-Source-Chrome-Extension zum Kopieren vorhandener Transkripte. Ditto unterstützt
   weitere Plattformen; YTTS konzentriert sich auf YouTube.
2. [Copy Youtube Transcript](https://alternativeto.net/software/copy-youtube-transcript/about/):
   direkter Bezug zu Kopieren von YouTube-Transkripten und Zeitstempeln.
3. [Youtube to Transcript](https://alternativeto.net/software/youtube-to-transcript/about/):
   nur für das Auslesen und Kopieren vorhandener YouTube-Transkripte vergleichbar. Die dort
   beworbenen zusätzlichen KI- und Transkriptionsfunktionen bietet YTTS nicht.
